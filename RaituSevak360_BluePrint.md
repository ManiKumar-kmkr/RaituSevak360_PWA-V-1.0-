Abstract
RaituSevak360 is an integrated digital agriculture platform designed to bring farmers, vendors, agricultural experts, and government employees together in one place. It provides farmers with access to a marketplace for buying and selling agricultural products, expert consultation, government schemes and subsidies, grievance support, agricultural camps, surveys, alerts, and useful agricultural information. Vendors can list their products, while experts and government personnel can communicate and provide relevant services to farmers.
The platform is developed as a Progressive Web Application (PWA) to provide easy access through mobile and web browsers. It also aims to incorporate AI-powered agricultural assistance in future versions for tasks such as answering agricultural questions, crop recommendations, disease identification, and personalized guidance. Overall, RaituSevak360 aims to create a single, connected digital ecosystem that makes agricultural services and information easier for farmers to access.
